# Requesting Medicaid TAF research access

Reviewed November 16, 2026. Official guidance governs; requirements and fees can change.

Start with [ResDAC RIF requests](https://resdac.org/research-identifiable-files-rif-requests). Ask which requester category fits your affiliation and whether you qualify as an independent applicant or need an organizational partner. Academic/nonprofit research and for-profit product development have different categories; describe commercialization plans accurately.

Proposed research: identify pediatric Medicaid care gaps and predict 90-day ED utilization to support coordination. Define population, states, calendar years, eligibility, outcomes, validation, intended publications and minimum necessary variables. An immigration endeavor statement is not a substitute for a research protocol.

Likely TAF files: Annual Demographic and Eligibility (DE), Other Services (OT), Inpatient (IP), Pharmacy (RX) if medication measures are justified; Annual Provider or Managed Care Plan files only if needed. Verify exact availability and state/year quality before selecting years. Separate Medicaid and CHIP eligibility explicitly.

Use the [Request Forms Generator](https://resdac.org/request-forms-generator) for your current packet, which may include study protocol, executive summary, specifications, DUA/signatories, funding, data-management/security documentation, and IRB/Common Rule and HIPAA waiver documentation. Do not self-declare an IRB exemption or assume it waives HIPAA requirements. Obtain the applicable determination from the reviewing body and verify CMS requirements.

[Preparation guidance](https://resdac.org/what-you-need-know-before-you-submit-request) says funding must be secured and explains security/IRB requirements. Ask for a written cost estimate using the [current fee guidance](https://resdac.org/cms-fee-information-research-identifiable-data). CCW VRDC is generally the access route; confirm current physical delivery eligibility rather than assuming local downloads are available.

[Submission instructions](https://resdac.org/cms-research-identifiable-request-process-timeline): email unsigned draft documents in their original formats to DataRequests@cms.hhs.gov; phone 1-844-685-3100. ResDAC helps revise the packet before final signatures and CMS review. Do not send patient records in an initial inquiry.

[CMS overview](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/cms-data/data-available-researchers) estimates RIF processing at 3–5 months; budget time for institutional preparation and revisions as well. Approval is not guaranteed. DUA access is study-specific and carries restrictions on users, purpose, security, sharing, output disclosure and retention/closure. CMS data and uncleared model outputs must not be put on public GitHub.

Subject: Feasibility inquiry — pediatric Medicaid TAF research request

Dear ResDAC team,

I am developing a research project to identify care gaps and predict 90-day emergency department utilization among children aged 0–17 enrolled in Medicaid, with the goal of supporting better care coordination. My current affiliation is [organization or independent researcher], and my anticipated funding is [source/status]. The intended outputs are [research publications and/or a commercial tool; describe accurately].

Could you advise on the appropriate requester category and organizational requirements; suitable TAF files, available years and state-level data quality; required IRB/HIPAA documentation; CCW VRDC access and approved Python/modeling options; and an estimated total first-year and renewal cost? I am considering starting with a limited number of states and would appreciate guidance on a minimum-necessary request.

Sincerely,
[Name and contact information]
